Welcome to TERECONET Journal of Education

TERECONET Journal of Education (TJE) is an international, open access, peer-reviewed journal committed to research that makes a difference. We exist to bridge research and practice — publishing rigorous, impactful scholarship that speaks to teachers, researchers, and everyone in between.

We believe the best educational research does not stay on the page. It raises voices too often unheard, informs real classrooms, and shapes how education is understood and practised around the world.

Publication in TJE is completely free for all authors, and all articles are freely available to everyone, everywhere.
